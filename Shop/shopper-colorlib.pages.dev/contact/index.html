Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions below.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for all unworn items in their original condition with tags attached. Returns are free for all orders.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping is available for 2-3 day delivery. Free shipping on orders over $100.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping times vary by location, typically 7-14 business days.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with tracking information. You can also track your order in your account Dashboard.
